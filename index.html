// About

I'm an independent engineering consultant based in St. Paul, MN, with over a decade of experience in regulated and safety-critical systems — primarily medical device risk management and aerospace system safety and reliability engineering.

Most recently I spent over three years at Abbott leading risk management for electrophysiology product development: RF ablation, pulsed field ablation, and cardiac imaging technologies. Before that, I was a reliability and system safety engineer at Honeywell Aerospace on commercial and defense programs, applying MIL-STD-882E, FTA, FMECA, and safety-critical function thread analysis across multiple platforms — and serving as Control Account Manager for program budgets and schedules.

I work well with teams that need experienced execution without added overhead: someone who can step in, get up to speed quickly, and deliver auditable, defensible work. Engagements are typically 20–40 hrs/week, 6–12 months, remote or hybrid.

Note: I am a co-founder of North Star Haptics, a hardware venture focused on tactile technology and human-interface design. That work is pre-revenue; my consulting practice operates wholly independently.
